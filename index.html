About CICR Cell

Creative & Innovative Cell in Robotics

CICR is the premier student-run robotics cell at the Jaypee Institute of Information Technology (JIIT), Sector-128, Noida.

Our cell serves as an incubator for creative engineering, hands-on robotics workshops, and competitive team projects in circuit layout, mechanical fabrication, and robotic controls.

Specializations Embedded Firmware, Mechanical CAD, Computer Vision & ROS
Lab Facility Robotics Research Lab, JIIT-128 Campus, Noida
